Behind the pages

A little about me.

I fell in love with writing in high school, starting with drama before finding my way into romance, poetry, and eventually psychological thrillers.

I'm a creative at heart. Whether it's books, poems, songs, or art, I love bleeding my soul onto pages. When I write, I visualize everything, every little detail, like a series playing out in my head.

My first completed novel was a psychological thriller inspired by reality, and somehow, every well-thought-out story since has led me back to the genre. Maybe I've found my niche, or maybe there's just something about the human mind that keeps pulling me in.

This is only the beginning. More stories to come.
